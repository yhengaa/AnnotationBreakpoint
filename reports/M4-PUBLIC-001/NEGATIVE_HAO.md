# Negative Hao5P demonstration

The accepted Hao5P public demonstration was negative. It used 600 selected
day-0 monocytes from six donors, artificial balanced condition labels, five
genes, and six stipulated ambiguous cells.

Across the six exact NME2/KLF2 gene-by-budget domains whose original claims
held, exhaustive enumeration found no allowed reassignment that crossed the
fixed effect threshold; those domains were stable only within their registered
six-cell domains. The other nine gene-by-budget claims were already unmet at
the original labels. All practical searches therefore returned either an
unresolved no-hit for an originally holding claim or
`original_claim_not_met`. No counterexample was reported.

This result does not establish population-wide robustness, a biological null,
or general search performance. It remains a deliberately preserved negative
public result. `status_counts.tsv` is an unchanged copy of the accepted status
table.
